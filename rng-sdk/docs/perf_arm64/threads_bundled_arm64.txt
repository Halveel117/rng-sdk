speedup    = median throughput at T threads / median at 1 thread.
efficiency = speedup / T. 100% is perfectly linear scaling; the last
             row is deliberately oversubscribed (2x hardware threads)
             and is EXPECTED to sit near 50% -- that is the scheduler
             time-slicing, not lock contention. A COLLAPSE there, or
             efficiency falling off well before the core count, would
             be the finding worth chasing.
spread     = max/min across repeats. Treat a cell above ~1.3x as noise-
             dominated and re-run on an idle machine -- see the load
             warning in rng_bench and OPEN_RESEARCH #7c(a).

Machines with heterogeneous cores (Apple P/E, Intel P/E) will show
efficiency sagging once the scheduler starts filling efficiency cores.
That is the hardware, not the library: report the core split alongside.
